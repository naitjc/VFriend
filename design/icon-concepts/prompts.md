# Generation prompts

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`; four independent generation calls, no reference images, no existing assets overwritten, opaque backgrounds.

## Common specification

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE original high-quality app icon concept for VFriend, an AI virtual companion app. Concept name for your understanding only: [name]. [concept below] Composition: square full-bleed app icon artwork, no surrounding mockup, no phone, no contact sheet. Keep the main symbol comfortably within the central 60 percent so Android circle and squircle masks cannot crop it. Restrained violet/periwinkle palette, strong contrast, clean crisp edges and thoughtful negative space. No words, no text, no Chinese, no typography except the V shape explicitly described, no watermark, no border frame, no copied existing app logo. Render as polished production-quality design exploration.

## Companion V

Two abstract companions forming the letter V: two small circular heads above two generous curved strokes converging into a rounded V. Warm, mature, gender-neutral companionship, extremely simple white geometric mark on a rich periwinkle and lavender background. Flat vector-like silhouette, a little asymmetric tenderness, no tiny details. Meaning is two people staying together, not a dating app.

## Conversation V

One bold, beautifully balanced speech bubble whose negative space forms a clear V. A single small chat tail, soft confident curves, white and pale lavender mark on deep violet. Minimal flat brand design, distinctive coherent silhouette, feels conversational and welcoming. Avoid generic double speech bubbles, avoid any existing brand resemblance.

## Friendly Face

A distinctive, restrained friendly face made of two small warm eyes and a broad rounded V-shaped smile. Soft dimensional matte forms with gentle depth, creamy white face elements on lavender purple, warm sophisticated friendly companion. Avoid the Discord logo, robot antennas, game controllers, animal ears, stock emoji, excessive cuteness.

## Quiet V

A single sculptural white V monogram with rounded ends and two gently interlocking ribbon strokes, subtly suggesting two paths meeting. Premium minimal flat-to-soft-dimensional brand mark, deep violet and airy lavender, calm intimate friendly feeling, excellent small-scale legibility. No circles above the V, no speech bubble, no face. Distinct from the other concepts.
